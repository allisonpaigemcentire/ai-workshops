29 Defaults

  • 01Be short. No preamble. Name a tradeoff. You decide.
  • 02Commit or open a PR only when you ask. A commit is not a push.
  • 03Never force-push to main. Follow the open repo’s hook rules.

29 / 33

Do not ask permission for read-only git, gh, or acli. Confirm before push, PR open or close, branch delete, or force-push unless a repo hook already asks.

30 Cost
